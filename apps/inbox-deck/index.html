Inbox Deck

Inbox Deck is a personal email-triage app. It shows your inbox one email at a time, with the sender's recent mail underneath, so you can trash, archive, snooze or skip quickly. It also offers sender-wide actions, rules and unsubscribe.

Every action is reversible: trashed mail goes to your Trash folder and is never permanently deleted by the app.

Inbox Deck is a private app for its developer's own use and is not offered to the public.
